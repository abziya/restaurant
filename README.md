# TalTech Restaurant Management System

A terminal-based restaurant management simulation program written in C. This project simulates daily restaurant operations, including client seating, reservations, table availability checks, a full 10-dish food menu, live ingredient inventory tracking, automated supplier restocking, billing with service fees, and daily revenue tracking.

---

## Features

* **Client Portal**: Manage table reservations and live table availability.
* **Food Menu System**: A balanced two-column menu featuring 10 unique dishes with preset prices.
* **Inventory & Stock Check**: Real-time tracking of ingredient quantities with status indicators (`[v]` for sufficient stock, `[!]` for low stock under 10 portions).
* **Automated Supplier Restocking**: Detects missing ingredients, calculates wholesale restocking costs, and prompts for administrative payment confirmation.
* **Billing & Payments**: Itemized receipts including dish price and service fees.
* **Staff Management**: Protected by a secure PIN interface (`1918`).
* **Interactive Day Flow**: Step-by-step confirmation prompts allowing management of multiple clients sequentially until ending the business day.
